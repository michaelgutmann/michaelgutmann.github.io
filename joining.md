# Joining my group

## Topics

I am particularly interested in machine-learning approaches to the following topics and questions:

- Optimal experimental design: Which actions teach us the most about an unknown system?  
- Systems design under uncertainty: Which actions help us design a system that we only partially understand?
- Simulation-based inference: How can we use a simulator to learn about the processes that generated the data?  
- Applications in engineering and the natural sciences: How can these methods speed up discovery and design in real scientific and engineering problems?

## Your profile

Ideally, you have strong mathematical foundations, with training in statistics or machine learning, including reinforcement learning and optimisation, and are fluent in the Python or Julia scientific stack.

## If interested

Please email me with:

1. a CV and transcripts
2. a short statement about what interests you most about my papers (2–3 paragraphs)
3. a short description of what you would like to do in my group (2–3 paragraphs)
4. your preferred start date
5. an indication of your funding situation

You do not need a fully worked-out proposal at this stage. The goal is to help me understand your interests and whether there might be a good match.

## PhD funding

There are several routes to a funded PhD at Edinburgh; see [here](https://informatics.ed.ac.uk/study-with-us/our-degrees/postgraduate-research-programmes-and-centres-doctoral-training) for an overview.

- The School of Informatics and the University offer competitive PhD scholarships. See [here](https://informatics.ed.ac.uk/study-with-us/our-degrees/postgraduate-research-programmes-and-centres-doctoral-training/postgraduate-research-funding-opportunities-0) and [here](https://registryservices.ed.ac.uk/student-funding/postgraduate).
- The School of Informatics has [fully funded Centres for Doctoral Training (CDTs)](https://informatics.ed.ac.uk/study-with-us/our-degrees/postgraduate-research-and-cdts/centres-doctoral-training-cdts). I am a member of the supervisor pool for the CDTs in [Biomedical Innovation](https://www.ai4biomed.io/), [Machine Learning Systems](https://informatics.ed.ac.uk/cdt-in-machine-learning-systems), and [Designing Responsible NLP](https://www.responsiblenlp.org/).

Application deadlines are typically between November and January; please check the sites above for exact dates.
